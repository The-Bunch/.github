## Build with hellobill

**Embed the whole journey.** Drop the move-in flow into your product — discovery, product selection, Letter of Authority, account creation — themed to your brand or ours. [`@hello-bill/sdk`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@hello-bill/sdk) in the browser, [`@hello-bill/node`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@hello-bill/node) on your server; auth, retries and idempotency handled.
→ [Embed SDK integration guide](https://hellobill.app/integrations/sdk)

**Or send us a lead — one POST.** No UI, no iframe: `POST /partner/sessions` creates a lead in Bill OS, and webhooks echo your `referral_id` + context back so your records stay linked.
→ Runnable examples: [`The-Bunch/leads`](https://github.com/The-Bunch/leads)

**Built on serious data.** UPRN-level knowledge of any UK property: meters (MPAN/MPRN), current suppliers, consumption, council tax band, address-level broadband availability, EPC, tenure — 20+ upstream UK sources, plus live quotes and switch submissions direct to suppliers.
→ [Data glossary](https://hellobill.app/integrations/datasources)
